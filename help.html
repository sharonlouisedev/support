Purchases and subscriptions

I subscribed, but the app still shows the paid features as locked.

Open the app and use Restore purchases on the subscription screen. If that does not help, check that you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy — Settings, then your name at the very top.

How do I see what I am subscribed to?

Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Everything you pay for through the App Store is listed there, with the renewal date.

How do I cancel?

Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, choose the app, then Cancel. Do this at least 24 hours before the period ends, or it renews. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription — this is the single most common reason for an unexpected charge.

How do I cancel during a free trial?

The same way, and at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Cancelling during a trial keeps access until the trial period is over and no payment is taken.

I would like a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and find the purchase. If Apple declines and you believe there has been a mistake, write to us and we will help where we can.

I was charged after deleting the app.

A subscription lives on your Apple ID, not in the app, so removing the app leaves it running. Cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, then contact Apple about the charge.

Using the app

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no password. Everything works straight away and works offline.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. There is no cloud copy and no account, which is why the app keeps working with no signal. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

Will my data move to a new phone?

If you set the new phone up from an iPhone or iCloud backup, yes — the app’s data is part of that backup. It does not sync live between two devices at the same time.

How do I keep a copy of something important?

Use the export or share option in the app and save the file to Files, or send it to yourself. Treat the app as a working tool rather than as long-term storage.

Privacy

What information do you collect?

No name, no email address, no account, and nothing you create in the app. What leaves the device relates only to confirming a purchase. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what that is.

Can you delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To have the subscription records associated with your device deleted, email us from that device and ask.
