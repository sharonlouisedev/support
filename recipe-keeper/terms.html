Cooking, measurements and allergies

This one matters more than the rest of this page. Recipe Keeper is a notebook and a calculator, not a cookery teacher and not a source of dietary or medical advice.

  • Times, temperatures and quantities — whether written by you, imported from a site, or read off a photograph — are shown as they were given. We do not check them, and text read from an image can be read wrongly.
  • Scaling servings and converting between metric and US measures is arithmetic. Most dishes take it well; baking, leavening, salting and anything with a chemical reaction in it often do not. Use judgement.
  • Ingredient categories, the shopping list and any ingredient information are conveniences, not a nutritional or allergen analysis. If an allergy, an intolerance or a medical condition is involved, check the ingredients themselves.
  • Follow safe food handling and cooking practice, particularly for meat, fish, eggs and preserving. When a recipe and food safety disagree, food safety wins.
