Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Wishlyst keeps what you save on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server of ours that your lists pass through.

The short version. We do not collect your data, because there is nowhere for it to go. Nothing you add is sent to us. We cannot see your wishes, your lists, your contacts, or what you bought for whom.

What the app stores, and where

  • On your device. Your wishlists, wishes, photos you attach, people you add, planned gifts, budgets and Secret Santa events are stored in the app's own database on your iPhone.

There is no account, no server and no cloud copy — the app has no backend at all. Two consequences worth knowing: it does not sync between your devices, and removing the app removes what it stored. Your iPhone's own encrypted backup is what carries the data to a new phone.

Contacts

If you choose to add people from Contacts, the app asks for permission first and then copies only the name and the birthday of the people you pick, plus an identifier used to avoid adding the same person twice.

  • Phone numbers, email addresses and postal addresses are never read and never stored.
  • Your contacts are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Declining the permission changes nothing else — people can be added by hand.

Photos

Photos you attach to a wish are picked by you and stored with that wish. The app does not browse your photo library and does not upload photos.

Reading a screenshot

When you add a wish from a screenshot, the text in the picture is recognised on your device, by the operating system's own text recognition. The picture is not sent anywhere for processing.

Links you paste

When you paste a product link, the app fetches that page — the same request your browser would make — and reads the information the shop publishes about it: the title, the picture, sometimes the price. The request goes to the shop, not to us. The shop can see that a request was made, as it can for any visitor.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Payment details are entered in Apple's own interface and are never seen by this app. We receive no card information and no name or address from a purchase.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party trackers or marketing tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, because none is collected.
  • No searching for other people by phone number or email — there is no such feature.

Sharing, when you choose it

Sharing a list, a wish or your profile creates a link, and the information travels inside the link itself — nothing is uploaded first. Whoever receives the link can open what it carries; nobody else can, because there is nothing stored anywhere to find. Nothing becomes public by default, and nothing about you is discoverable by strangers.

Secret Santa

The draw happens on the organiser's device. The result travels as a single link in which every participant's pair is sealed separately, so a phone can open only its own pair — reading the whole message tells you nothing. No participant list, no pairing and no message reaches us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

One honest limitation: the organiser's device performed the draw, so the organiser could in principle see who got whom. The app neither stores nor displays that, but it is a property of the app, not of mathematics.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. There is no copy elsewhere to delete, and no account to close.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
