If the button did nothing

The app may not be installed yet, or your messenger trimmed the link. Copy the code below, open Wishlyst on your iPhone, go to the Santa tab and tap Join with a code.

What happens next

  • You join the exchange from the link above.
  • You send the organiser one short reply, so your result can be sealed for you.
  • When everyone is in, the organiser draws names and sends one message to the group.
  • Your phone opens the one part of it that belongs to you — and nobody else's.

One honest note

The draw runs on the organiser's iPhone, so whoever set the exchange up could see every pair. The app doesn't hide that from you and doesn't claim otherwise. What it does guarantee is the other direction: the message sent to the group reveals nothing to anyone but its intended reader.
